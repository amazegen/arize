Arize Student Edition: operating checklist

More Life Consulting · October 29, 2026 · Internal working procedure, not a legal/compliance certification.

Before enrolling a real school

  1. Record the actual school name and verify its authorized administrator, teacher and counselor identities. Do not treat a registration form or email domain as proof of school authority.
  2. Record the approved school/guardian consent process and current disclosures. Enable only collection and reporting covered by that authorization.
  3. Configure and verify the real counselor recipient. An accepted email receipt does not prove that a counselor read the request. Chat does not create automatic counselor alerts.
  4. Invite students as pending, verify their school assignment, then separately activate enrollment after consent review. Never reuse an adult staff account as a student account.
  5. Test a real invitation, password setup, correct role routing and counselor delivery with designated adult testers before the first student invitation. No student private chats should be used as test fixtures.

Privileged account security

Authenticator setup is available at /student/security. Existing accounts are not forced into MFA during rollout. After an account verifies a factor, staff API and database access require AAL2; the next password-only sign-in goes through a verification screen.

  • The account holder scans the private QR and enters their own current code. Never send QR codes, secrets or codes to engineering chat or logs.
  • Assign a second verified administrator and agree on an identity-verified recovery procedure before mandatory MFA for all staff is introduced.
  • No automated MFA reset or recovery bypass is provided by the app. Do not remove a real factor on a verbal request without identity verification and an approved recovery process.

Data requests and retention: decisions still required

No retention duration has been invented and no automatic deletion job has been enabled. Curtis and each school need to name the request owner and approve the retention/disposal rules before this item can be called complete.

  1. Agree on a verified channel for access, correction, deletion and consent-withdrawal requests. Do not ask students to include chat contents or sensitive details in ordinary email.
  2. Verify the requester and school authority, assign a case reference, record the requested scope and approval, and review applicable obligations with the school's authorized privacy/legal contact.
  3. Before deletion, inventory Auth records, private chats, goals, profiles, opt-in responses, counselor requests, enrollment/notification/follow-up audits and usage counters. Some counters are not Auth-cascaded. Check FK dependencies and backup handling; do not promise that deleting an Auth user alone erases every copy.
  4. Decide how historical approved reports should behave after consent withdrawal, school feature withdrawal or correction. Current reports are frozen snapshots, not automatically recalculated or deleted.
  5. Apply only the approved scoped action, verify the result, record who performed it, and notify the requester through the verified channel. Irreversible deletion still needs explicit approval at execution time.

AI request records retain request identifiers, keyed fingerprints, status and moderated replies for retry protection. Raw blocked input and Test Lab prompts are not saved in those records. These metadata and safe-reply copies must be included in the approved retention policy.

Provider and deployment checks

  • Use only the isolated Student Supabase backend and arize-student Vercel project. Keep adult-app deployments, secrets and roles separate.
  • Server-only credentials must never be placed in a NEXT_PUBLIC_ variable, copied into source, shown in dashboards or included in logs.
  • The OpenAI request uses store:false. This is not proof of zero provider retention; verify the actual account retention/eligibility settings and school disclosure separately.
  • Confirm the actual Supabase backup coverage and perform an isolated restore drill. Do not assume that a plan name proves a tested recovery process.
  • Assign an incident owner, contact route, alert thresholds and response procedure. Review provider availability, quotas, credential compromise and unauthorized-access attempts without logging student text.

Chat failures and retry behavior

  • Browser accounts can read their own saved conversations but cannot reserve/write a coach turn directly.
  • The server checks fresh enrollment/current notice, reserves a durable request budget, moderates input, then stores one approved user turn. Replies are moderated before storage.
  • Current clients retain a stable request ID across ambiguous retries. Completed retries return the prior result, not another stored turn. A changed draft starts a new request.
  • If a turn is already pending, wait and check saved history. If a saved turn failed to receive a reply, do not silently resend its input. Start a new message after checking the actual saved state.
  • Limits are 60 pre-provider student requests/day and 10/minute, with the existing 30 saved coaching turns/day and 5/minute. Operator tests have 100 pre-provider requests/day and 10/minute. Limits are per account and use database state, not per-server memory.

Reporting and publication

  • Reports require an approved school scope and a completed month. Small-group withholding and count ranges remain in place; missing data is not zero.
  • Attendance remains class-level totals. Repeated student-sessions are not unique students and are not a measure of chronic absenteeism.
  • Published attendance periods are frozen. A correction needs an approved, audited report/version process; do not edit around the guard or silently change a published source.
  • Approved internal program reports are not automatic permission for public case studies. Do not claim that Arize caused improved attendance or wellbeing from these operational counts alone.
