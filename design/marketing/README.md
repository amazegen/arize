# Adult marketing design adaptation

Reference: Jadon's [approved welcome / landing frame 44:452](https://www.figma.com/design/WKKcQuHiUXj9ujmglwnYOc/arize-prototype--mockups?node-id=44-452), reviewed through high-fidelity design context and its screenshot on 2026-11-22.

That frame is a welcome card, not a completed long-form marketing site. Phase 6 extends its cream canvas, Nunito typography, orange pill actions, green-soft chips, white 24px cards and original flower logo into the approved hero/features/pricing/FAQ/lead-capture scope. It reuses `styles/arize-theme.css` and the existing adult button/brand/focus styles; it does not introduce another token system or edit Student CSS.

The reference's Student Edition chip and mascot/tagline placeholders are not marketing copy or finished assets. They are replaced with adult positioning and the existing tagline. No invented lion, testimonial, customer number, outcome claim, full-screen Figma screenshot or temporary asset URL is shipped.

The original `8f044.png` logo exactly matches the local Phase 5 asset `public/design/founder/logo.png` (1315×1197 PNG). The header/footer render it at 36×33, the welcome card at 72×66, with containment. All three loaded at those intended browser dimensions. The yellow background shape is decoration, not a replacement for the missing lion asset.

Reviewed at 320, 375, 768 and 1280 CSS pixels. The introductory card is labeled as an introduction, not a screen recording or fabricated live account. Pricing is generated from the real membership constants rather than Figma sample prices.
